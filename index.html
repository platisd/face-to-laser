What is in the .zip file?
*_combined.svg
Picture and cut line in one file, at the right size. Try this first.
*_engrave.png
Only the picture. Black dots = burn. Has the dots per inch inside, so it opens at the right size.
*_cut.svg, *_cut.dxf
Only the cut line, as a red line. Same size and position as the picture.
*_settings.json
The settings used. Load it again with Load settings.

In Falcon Design Space: set the picture to engrave and the red line to cut. The speed and power for your material are set there, not here.
